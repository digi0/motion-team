Big creator deals happen in DMs. Smaller ones need a steady routine. Here's a weekly rhythm I'd be glad to help run, with the team setting the direction.

How I'd help

The Nov 12 handoff

The giveaway ends.
the reasons to stay can't.

Subscriptions earn Urus entries right now, so some people subscribed for the drawing. When it closes, a bench of creators gives them a new reason to keep the box. Starting now means that bench is ready on day one.

Now to Oct 31

Recruit while it's loud

Sign the first 40 Starter faces while giveaway attention peaks. Every new code is another reason to talk about MOTION.

Nov 1 to 12

Ride the final push

Creators post "lock in with me" content alongside the last bonus-entry windows, so the spike also builds their audiences.

Nov 13

The drawing is done. Creators carry the routine content: "what I got done today", flavor rankings, pre-gym and long-shift pouch checks.

December

Promote and plan

First tier promotions, paid collabs for the top codes, and the next giveaway built around creator teams instead of one face.
